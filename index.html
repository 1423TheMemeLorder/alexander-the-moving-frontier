READING THE EVIDENCE

Sources, uncertainty & scope

This is a source-aware reconstruction of major battles, sieges, assaults, enemy attacks, strategic decisions, winter quarters and attested halts connected with Alexander’s career. Ancient narratives do not preserve every daily camp or skirmish.

Exact

Securely dated or closely located by the evidence.

Approximate

Month, season or modern coordinate is an informed estimate.

Disputed

Date or ancient place identification remains debated.

Primary and reference works

    Dates are BCE. Route lines follow broad, source-informed campaign corridors; intermediate waypoints are approximate and do not claim to reproduce exact ancient roads. The editable historical-region polygons—including Illyria—are broad orientation zones, not claims of fixed or universally agreed ancient borders. Modern borders and labels come from the selected basemap.
    MAP GUIDE

    How to explore

    • Play animates the route and keeps the moving dot centred on the campaign line.
    • Drag the timeline to jump to any episode.
    • Select a marker for Alexander’s decision, outcome, camp note and evidence confidence.
    • Search a person to open a profile, then choose one of their key episodes to locate it on the map.
    • Filter battles, sieges, hostile actions, decisions, camps and political events from the top bar.
    • Collapse the campaign log with the edge button or the B key.
    • Historical regions opens editable geographic polygons, including Illyria. Click a region to recolour it; colours are saved automatically.
    • Switch basemaps with the layer control in the map’s upper-right corner.
